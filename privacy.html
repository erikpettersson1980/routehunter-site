Route Hunter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Route Hunter is made by Le Peloton, Sweden ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores, where, and why. Short version: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Most of your data never leaves your phone.

Data on your device

Your badge checklist, power and weight, units, and the route you plan to ride tonight are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on for Route Hunter, the same data is kept in sync through your own iCloud account, which we cannot read.

Strava (optional)

If you connect Strava, Route Hunter reads your rides to find which routes you completed. To do this our server stores:

We storeWhy
Your Strava athlete IDTo know which rides are yours
Strava access keys, encryptedTo read new rides when Strava tells us you uploaded one
Matched routes: route, date and activity IDTo tick the route off in your app
A push notification token, if you allow notificationsTo tell you a route was ticked off

We do not store GPS tracks, power, heart rate, photos or your name. Your Strava data is shown only to you, is never sold or shared, and is never used for advertising, marketing or training AI models.

Deleting it: tap "Disconnect Strava" in the app, or revoke Route Hunter in your Strava settings. Everything we store about you is deleted immediately.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Pro; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt, not your name or email.

Notifications

The "ride tonight" reminder is created on your phone. Push notifications about ticked routes are sent through Apple's push service (via Expo's push service). You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and object to its use. Disconnecting Strava deletes everything on our side. For anything else, contact us at routehunter@shuvit-dev.se. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

The legal basis for the Strava connection is your consent (you can withdraw it by disconnecting) and our contract with you to provide the feature.

Children

Route Hunter is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. Significant changes are announced in the app.
