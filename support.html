How are the times calculated?

From your power and weight and each route's elevation profile, with a physics model of air drag, rolling resistance and gravity. "Group" assumes drafting; "Solo" is about 8 % slower. Real rides vary.

Strava didn't tick off a route I rode

  • Check that the ride reached Strava and is a virtual ride.
  • Routes are matched using the route's Strava segment. If your ride has no segment efforts (for example with some privacy settings), it can't be matched.
  • Rides are checked when Strava tells us about them, usually within minutes. Open the app to sync.
  • You can always tick a route by hand.

Restore Pro on a new phone

Open the You tab, tap Pro and choose "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Delete my data

Everything except the Strava connection lives on your phone (and your iCloud, if enabled). Deleting the app removes it. Tap "Disconnect Strava" to delete everything on our server.

Cancel a subscription

In iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Route Hunter.
