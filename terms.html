What the app does

Route Hunter helps you plan and track rides on virtual cycling routes. Times, XP figures and interval suggestions are estimates from a model and from community reports. Real rides vary with drafting, power-ups, pacing and game updates.

Strava

Connecting Strava is optional and follows Strava's own terms. Automatic ticking depends on Strava and on the segment data in your rides; we cannot guarantee that every completed route is detected. You can always tick routes by hand.
